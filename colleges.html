Good Deeds Day in Colleges

Colleges are one of the fastest-growing parts of the Good Deeds Day India movement. Campuses brought youth-led energy to city-wide Good Deeds Day Fests in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Chennai, where students ran stalls, led volunteering activities, and connected with causes alongside NGOs and citizens.
